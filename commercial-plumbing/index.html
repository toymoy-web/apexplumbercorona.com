Shared Buildings.

Leaks In Apartment Buildings, Plazas And Shared Lines

A leak in a multi-unit property often shows up in a different place than it starts. Water in a parking lot may come from a line under the slab.

We locate the source with listening and pressure tools, then plan the repair around tenants and access. Small multi-unit properties such as triplexes and fourplexes are handled in the same way.

Fire protection piping is a separate trade. If water is coming from a fire line, we help confirm that the domestic plumbing is not the cause, then the right contractor takes over.

Why This Matters In Corona.

A Mix Of Older And New Commercial Stock

Corona's commercial corridors include both older retail buildings along the original downtown core and newer commercial development near the 91/15 interchange. Older commercial buildings often carry galvanized or aging cast iron lines that behave differently under commercial-scale water use than the newer PVC and copper systems in recently built retail centers. That is worth knowing before assuming a fix that worked at one property will behave the same way at another. All commercial work we perform follows standard UPC and California cross-connection control requirements for backflow prevention, not an informal in-house standard.

Commercial plumbing code, grease trap requirements, backflow testing schedules, and multi-tenant access logistics are genuinely different from a single-family repair call. After-Hours Calls.

Floor Drain Overflows, Restroom Shutdowns And Lift Pumps

When a restroom cannot be used or a floor drain overflows, the business stops. Call right away and have these ready.

Shut The Main If Safe

If water is spreading and the main valve is reachable, close it. Note where it is for the technician.

Keep Foot Traffic Away

Block off wet or sewage-affected areas so customers and staff stay clear.

Name A Site Contact

Tell the dispatcher who can grant access and where the shutoff valves are located.

Photograph The Problem

Wide and close photos help with work orders and property records.

Restaurants and shops often have floor drains, grease-prone lines and lift pumps. We clear the line first, then check the cause. Related work is on our drain cleaning page and leak detection page.

FAQs.

Common Questions

Can you work from our property management software's work orders?

Yes, we can accept work orders directly and confirm completion back through your process.

Do you handle grease trap pumping for restaurants?

Yes, both routine scheduled pumping and emergency clearing when needed.

Do you perform annual backflow testing?

Yes, and we provide documentation for your records and any required reporting.

Can you service multiple properties under one account?

Yes, we can set up recurring service across a portfolio with consistent scheduling and pricing.

Do you carry commercial liability coverage?

Yes, and documentation is available on request for your property's vendor requirements.

Do you take emergency calls for apartment buildings and plazas?

Yes. Our line runs 24/7, and the dispatcher asks about the building, the affected area and who can give access.

Can you find a leak under a parking lot or a slab?

Yes. We use the same listening and pressure tools as on a home, scaled to the property. Larger sites take longer to isolate, and we explain the plan first.

Do you work on new construction plumbing?

Yes. We take on new construction plumbing for small and mid-size projects. Share the plans and timeline when you call.

Can you check a floor drain that overflows in a shop or restaurant?

Yes. We clear the line and look for the cause, whether that is buildup, a damaged section or a failed lift pump.
