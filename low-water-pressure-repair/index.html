Signs Of A Pressure Problem.

What Low Pressure Looks Like At Home

Weak Flow In The Shower

A trickle at the showerhead, especially when someone else runs water, points to a supply issue.

Slow Taps In Every Room

When every fixture is weak, the cause is upstream of the house plumbing.

Pressure That Swings

Pressure that rises and falls through the day often points to a regulator or a leak.

Hot Side Only Is Weak

Weak hot water with normal cold flow points to the water heater or its fittings.

How We Diagnose.

A Gauge Reading Comes Before Any Repair

We attach a gauge to an outdoor hose bib and read the static pressure. Then we compare it with the flow at several fixtures. The numbers show whether the problem is the supply, the regulator or the pipe.

A reading that is too low points to the main valve, the regulator or a leak. A reading that is too high can strain fixtures and fittings, so a high number matters as much as a low one.

Common Causes.

What Usually Sits Behind Weak Pressure

Partly Closed ValveA main valve or meter valve that was not opened fully after earlier work.
Failed RegulatorA worn pressure regulator can choke flow or let pressure creep up.
Clogged Aerator Or CartridgeMineral scale blocks small openings and weakens one fixture.
Corroded PipeOld galvanized pipe narrows from the inside, which is covered on our repiping page.
A Hidden LeakWater escaping before it reaches the taps lowers pressure. See our leak detection page.
Water Heater SedimentScale in the tank or fittings can weaken hot water only.

Corona Factors.

Hard Water, Older Pipe And Pressure Zones

Corona water runs moderately hard, so scale builds up inside aerators, cartridges and older metal pipe. About one in four homes here were built before 1980, and many of them have original supply pipe.

Pressure can also vary with elevation. Homes on higher ground such as parts of Sierra del Oro may see a different baseline than those near the Circle. A gauge reading removes the guesswork.

No Water Right Now.

When The Taps Run Dry

No water at all is worth treating as urgent. Check whether the house main valve is fully open and whether neighbors have water. If they do not, the issue is likely at the utility. If they do, call us.

Check The Main Valve

Turn it counterclockwise until it stops. A valve that is halfway closed is a common cause.

Look At The Meter

If the dial is spinning and nothing is on, water is escaping before it reaches the taps.

Check Nearby Homes

If the whole street is out, the utility is the likely source.

Call The 24/7 Line

A leaking or broken line may need immediate attention.

FAQs.

Common Questions

Why is my water pressure low in the whole house?

A partly closed main valve, a failing regulator, a hidden leak or corroded pipe can all do it. A gauge reading at an outdoor hose bib separates those causes.

Can too much pressure be a problem?

Yes. High pressure strains fixtures, supply lines and the water heater, and it can cause banging pipes. A regulator that has drifted high is a common cause.

Why is only my hot water weak?

Sediment or scale in the water heater or its fittings usually causes it. The water heater repair page covers that case, and we start by checking the shutoff valves.

What if the pressure dropped suddenly?

A sudden drop often means a closed valve, a leak or a failing regulator. Check the main valve, then call so we can read the pressure.

Is no water at all an emergency?

It can be. If neighbors have water and you do not, call right away, because a broken line or a closed valve is the likely cause.
