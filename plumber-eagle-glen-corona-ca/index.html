General Plumbing Information.

What Two Decades Can Mean For Plumbing

If a home was built around the year 2000 and its original tank water heater was never replaced, that tank is well past a typical lifespan. Most tank heaters last roughly a decade or more, so the install date and any change in how fast the water heats are worth tracking.

Other parts that wear with time in any house include shutoff valves under sinks, toilet fill valves, hose bibs and the pressure regulator near the main. A regulator that has drifted can cause high pressure, which stresses fixtures, or low pressure, which shows up as weak showers.

Any home with a landscaped yard has outdoor irrigation and hose connections. A wet patch in a yard can come from sprinklers or from a supply line, and a pressure check helps separate the two.

Questions From Eagle Glen Homeowners

Do you serve Eagle Glen homes near the golf course?

Yes. Eagle Glen is part of Corona and falls inside our service area. Give us your street when you call.

Which ZIP code is Eagle Glen in?

Listing sources place it in 92881 and 92883. If you are unsure, your mailing address is the best guide.

Should I replace a water heater that is about 20 years old?

A tank that old is past the typical range, so many owners plan a replacement. We can inspect yours and say what we see.

Why is the water pressure different in my house?

Causes include a regulator setting, a partly closed valve, a clogged fixture or a line problem. A gauge reading at a hose bib is the first test.
