Local Plumbing Factors.

Why Chino Hills Homes Call For A Different Approach

Built In The 1980s And Early 1990s

Housing development here boomed through the 1980s and early 1990s, which means plenty of original plumbing is now 30 to 40 years old. Water heaters, supply lines, and shutoff valves from that era are reaching the stage where a pattern of repairs can point to a repipe.

Hillside Lots And Pressure Swings

Much of the city is rolling hills, and a house at the bottom of a slope sees higher water pressure than one at the top. A pressure regulator that has quietly failed can stress every fixture, so we test pressure first.

Master-Planned Neighborhoods With Rules

Subdivisions such as Butterfield Ranch, Fairfield Ranch, Payne Ranch, and Rolling Ridge come with community standards. We coordinate with association rules on visible exterior work like irrigation backflow devices.

Los Serranos And Sleepy Hollow

Los Serranos began as a 1920s golf country club subdivision, and Sleepy Hollow sits up in Carbon Canyon. These older, differently built corners need a different first look than the tracts around them.

Emergency Prep.

If You Have A Burst Pipe Or Active Leak In Chino Hills

Hillside plumbing can send water a long way downhill before you spot it. Here is what to do in the first few minutes.

Shut Off The Main Valve

Find the main shutoff, often at the front of the house or near the garage in Chino Hills tracts, and turn it clockwise to stop water to the whole house.

Cut Power To Wet Areas

If water is reaching an outlet, panel, or appliance, switch off that area at the breaker box first, then keep clear of the wet zone.

Clear The Immediate Area

Move belongings off wet floors, and check below a leak on a sloped lot or a lower level where water may have collected.

Call Our 24/7 Line

Tell us your neighborhood and whether the water is coming from supply, drain, or the water heater so we can bring the right parts.
