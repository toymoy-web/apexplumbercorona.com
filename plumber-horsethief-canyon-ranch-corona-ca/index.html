General Plumbing Information.

When A Neighbor Has A Plumbing Problem

In a tract of nearly two thousand homes, news travels. A neighbor repipes, replaces a water heater or finds a slab leak, and the question follows. The honest answer is that houses built by the same builder can still differ in remodels, water use, past repairs and how well each has been maintained.

A single leak does not mean a repipe is needed. Repeated leaks in different spots, discolored water or falling pressure across the whole house are the signs that justify comparing repairs against replacement. Leak detection and a pressure test give the data for that decision.

Tank water heaters, shutoff valves and fill valves are also common calls in any large tract. Keeping a note of the install date on the heater makes later decisions easier.
