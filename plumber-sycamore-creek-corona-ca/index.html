General Plumbing Information.

Pressure, Elevation And Your Association

Homes near hillsides can sit at noticeably different elevations on the same water system. In general, higher ground tends to see lower pressure and lower ground higher pressure. A pressure gauge on a hose bib gives a real number instead of a guess.

If a shower is weak at only one fixture, the cause is often local, such as a clogged aerator or a partly closed valve. If every fixture is weak, look at the regulator, the main valve or the service line.

Many master-planned communities handle front yard landscaping and some common areas through the association. Before paying for a wet spot outside, check whether the line is yours or common. A leak detection visit can show which side of the property line the water is coming from.

FAQs.

Questions From Sycamore Creek Homeowners

Is Sycamore Creek inside the City of Corona?

Sources describe it as a master-planned community in Corona, with listings in ZIP 92883.

Does the HOA handle plumbing problems?

Associations differ. Check your governing documents for which lines are common and which are yours.

Why is my water pressure low only upstairs?

Local causes include a clogged aerator or partly closed valve. If it affects the whole house, a regulator or main problem is more likely.

Can you test pressure at my home?

Yes. A gauge on an outdoor hose bib gives a quick reading, and we explain what the number means.
