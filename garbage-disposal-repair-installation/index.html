Common Issues.

What Usually Brings Us Out

Humming But Not Spinning

Usually a jammed impeller. Often resolvable with a reset, though a foreign object may need to be manually cleared first.

No Sound At All

Could be a tripped reset button, a electrical issue, or a fully failed motor, each requiring a different fix.

Leaking From The Bottom

Typically a failed internal seal, which usually means the unit is due for replacement rather than repair.

Leaking At The Sink Connection

Often just a loose mounting or a failed gasket where the unit connects to the sink, a straightforward fix.

Why This Matters In Corona.

Hard Water And Disposal Wear

Corona's moderately hard water contributes to internal component wear over a disposal's life, similar to its effect on other fixtures. Beyond water hardness, disposal failures are just as often caused by what goes down them, fibrous foods, grease, and large volumes at once are the most common causes of jamming and premature motor wear regardless of water hardness. A disposal fed carefully typically outlasts one used as a catch-all for anything that fits down the drain.

Repair Or Replace.

When Each Makes Sense

Repair Usually Fits When

The unit is jammed, has a tripped reset, or a fixable connection issue with the sink or dishwasher line.

Replacement Usually Fits When

The motor has failed, the unit is leaking internally, or it's old enough that continued repairs no longer make sense.

Sizing A New Unit

Horsepower and feed type are matched to how the kitchen actually uses the disposal, not just the cheapest available model.

Same-Day Installation

Most standard disposal installations, including removing an old unit, are completed in a single visit.

Disposal Jammed Right Now?

Many jams resolve with a quick reset, but a persistent one is worth a proper look before it damages the motor.

Not Sure If It's Worth Fixing.

A Quick Way To Tell

A jam or connection issue on an otherwise working unit is almost always worth repairing. A unit that's leaking internally, making grinding noises without spinning, or fully silent despite power is usually at or near end of life, and replacement is the better value at that point.

Pricing Factors.

What Affects Cost

Repair Vs ReplaceA jam clear or connection fix costs far less than a full unit replacement.
Horsepower And FeaturesHigher horsepower and quieter-operation models cost more than standard units.
Electrical WorkA disposal without an existing dedicated circuit may need minor electrical work during installation.
Dishwasher ConnectionUnits feeding a dishwasher drain line have an added connection point to account for.
Mounting Assembly ConditionA worn or damaged mounting assembly found during installation may need replacement alongside the unit.

A worn-out disposal is a maintenance and replacement cost, not an insurance matter. A sudden leak that damaged the cabinet or flooring beneath it is a separate situation.

FAQs.

Common Questions

Can I reset it myself before calling?

Yes, the reset button on the unit's underside is worth trying first. If it trips again immediately, that points to a jam or electrical issue worth having checked.

What shouldn't go down a disposal?

Fibrous vegetables, grease, coffee grounds in large amounts, and bones are the most common causes of jamming and premature wear.

How long does installation take?

Most standard installations, including removing the old unit, are completed in under an hour.

Is a louder disposal a sign of a problem?

Not necessarily on its own, but a sudden change in sound, especially grinding or rattling, is worth having checked.

Can a disposal be added where one has never existed?

Yes, provided the sink has the right drain configuration or can be adapted. We can assess this during scheduling.

Do you haul away the old unit?

Yes, removal and disposal of the old unit is included with any replacement installation.
