Corona Plumbing, By The Numbers

Why Corona Homes Call Us First

Corona sits at the junction of the 91 and 15 freeways in western Riverside County, against the Santa Ana Mountains. The city earned the nickname "Circle City" from its original 1886 traffic circle, and that older core still runs a mix of pipe materials that newer neighborhoods do not.

Older Tract Homes

Much of central and south Corona was built out in the 1970s with copper supply lines that are now 50+ years old and prone to pinhole leaks and slab leaks. We carry acoustic and electronic leak-detection gear built for exactly this housing stock.

Newer Developments

Areas like Dos Lagos and Eastvale-adjacent tracts run PEX and newer copper, so the common calls skew toward water heater sizing, fixture installs, and warranty-adjacent repairs rather than slab leaks.

Earthquake Shut-Off Valves

Corona sits near active Southern California fault lines. An automatic earthquake gas shut-off valve is a real, California-specific upgrade that several local competitors already install, and it is worth asking about if your home does not have one.

Hard Water & Pool Country

Corona Department of Water & Power delivers moderately hard water, which shortens water heater life over time. Corona's suburban housing stock also includes a large share of backyard pools. That is why pool leak detection is a real, standing local call category here.

What To Do Before We Arrive

If You Have An Active Leak Or Burst Pipe

Find The Shutoff

The main shutoff valve is usually near the street-side water meter or where the line enters the house. Turning it clockwise stops the flow while you wait for a technician.

Cut Power Near Water

If water is pooling near an outlet, panel, or water heater, shut off power to that circuit at the breaker box before touching anything electrical.

Document For Insurance

Photograph the leak, the affected area, and the water meter reading before cleanup starts. Most homeowner policies want this before they will process a claim.

Know What We Don't Cover

We handle the plumbing repair itself. Structural drying, mold remediation, and content restoration are a separate service category, and we can point you to that page if the same event caused water damage.

FAQs

Common Questions About Corona Plumbing Service

Do you charge for emergency calls after hours?

Our emergency line runs 24/7 at no extra "after-hours" line fee, though emergency dispatch and non-standard-hours labor is priced differently from a scheduled daytime visit. You get the number before any work starts.

Do you offer free estimates?

Yes, for non-emergency work. If a pipe is actively leaking or you have no water, we prioritize stopping the damage first and confirm pricing on-site before repairs begin.

Why do so many Corona homes get slab leaks?

A large share of Corona's housing stock was built in the 1960s-1970s with copper supply lines routed under the concrete slab. After 50+ years, that copper is a common source of pinhole leaks, which is why slab leak detection is one of our highest-volume calls.

Do you work on commercial properties?

Yes, including property-management and multi-tenant work orders. Commercial jobs typically need a site visit to scope before pricing, since access and code requirements vary by building.
