Signs You Need It.

When Repair Stops Making Sense

No Hot Water

A tank that has fully stopped heating, or a tankless unit throwing repeated error codes, usually costs more to keep patching than to replace.

Rusty Or Discolored Water

Rust-colored hot water is often the tank's inner lining failing, which is not a repairable part.

Popping Or Rumbling Noises

Sediment buildup at the bottom of an older tank causes this, and it gets worse, not better, over time.

Age Over 10 Years

Most standard tank water heaters are built for a 10-12 year service life. Past that, a repair is a temporary fix on a unit near the end regardless.

Tank Failure.

When A Water Heater Starts Flooding The Garage

Water coming from the base of the tank is the signal to act fast. Do these four things, then call.

Close The Cold Inlet

Turn the valve above the heater clockwise to stop new water from feeding the leak.

Cut Power Or Gas

Switch off the breaker for an electric unit, or turn the gas control to off or pilot for a gas unit.

Keep Clear Of The Water

Standing water near a plugged-in appliance or an outlet is a shock risk. Stay out until the power is off.

Do Not Open The Drain Valve

A hot tank can scald. Let the technician handle draining.

A leak from a fitting or relief valve may be repairable, so we start with a diagnosis. A leak from the tank itself is the point where replacement makes sense. Our repair page explains the repair side.

Replacement timing depends on stock and the day's schedule. You will hear a plain answer on the phone about what is possible.

Why This Matters In Corona.

Corona's Hard Water Shortens Tank Life

Corona's municipal water supply runs moderately hard, and mineral scale builds up inside a tank faster than it would with softer water. That scale insulates the heating element from the water it is supposed to heat, so the unit works harder and wears out sooner. It is a real, common reason a 10-year-old tank in Corona is further along than a 10-year-old tank somewhere with softer water. A water softener installed ahead of the tank extends the life of whatever unit you choose, tank or tankless.

Tank Vs Tankless.

Which Fits Your Corona Home

Older Corona neighborhoods with smaller utility closets often stay with a tank simply for space reasons, while newer builds and larger households increasingly ask about tankless for the endless hot water and smaller footprint. Neither is a universal right answer. We size the recommendation to your household's actual hot water demand and your home's existing gas or electrical service, not a default upsell.

The Real Trade-Offs.

Tank Vs Tankless, Honestly

Traditional Tank

  • Lower upfront cost.
  • Simpler installation in most homes.
  • Hot water supply is limited by tank size.
  • Typical 10-12 year lifespan.

Tankless

  • Continuous hot water on demand.
  • Lower standby energy cost.
  • Compact wall-mounted unit.
  • Typical 15-20 year lifespan with regular flushing.

Fuel And Type Changes.

Swapping Gas For Electric Or Choosing A Different Type

Some callers want to change fuel type or move from a tank to tankless during the replacement. That can mean new venting, a new gas line or an electrical upgrade.

We handle those changes, including heat pump water heaters. Mention your current fuel type and the room the unit sits in when you call, so the quote reflects any venting or electrical work. See also our tankless service page.

FAQs.

Common Questions

How fast can you install a new unit?

Most replacements are completed the same day for calls before 2pm, subject to unit availability.

Do you haul away the old tank?

Yes, removal and disposal is included in every replacement, tank or tankless.

Is the price locked before work starts?

Yes, you approve a flat price before any work begins.

Can I switch from tank to tankless?

Usually yes, though it may require additional gas line or electrical work depending on the model, which we will scope during the visit.

Does a leaking water heater always need to be replaced?

No. A leak at a fitting or relief valve can often be repaired. A leak from the tank body means the tank is failing and replacement is the answer.

What should I shut off if my water heater is leaking?

Close the cold water inlet valve above the unit and switch off the power or gas control. Then call so the technician can see the situation.

Can I swap my gas water heater for an electric or heat pump unit?

Yes. It can need electrical work and changes to venting, and we quote those along with the unit.

What does water heater replacement cost?

The price depends on the unit type, size, venting and any code upgrades. You get the number before work starts.
