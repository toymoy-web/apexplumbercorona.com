When Jetting Is The Right Call.

Signs Snaking Isn't Enough

A Clog That Returns Within Weeks

If snaking clears it but the same clog comes back on a schedule, the pipe wall itself likely has buildup that a cable alone doesn't remove.

Grease-Heavy Kitchen Lines

Restaurant and heavy-use kitchen lines accumulate grease along the entire pipe wall, not just at one point, which jetting is specifically built to clear.

Root Intrusion In Older Lines

Roots that have worked into a pipe joint spread along the interior wall. Jetting cuts and clears root growth more thoroughly than cabling.

Slow Drainage Across Multiple Fixtures

Gradual buildup narrowing a shared line often shows up as multiple slow drains before it becomes a full clog.

Why This Comes Up In Corona.

Older Lines And Mature Landscaping

Corona's older neighborhoods run original clay and cast iron sewer laterals in places, and decades-old trees near those lines make root intrusion a real, recurring issue. Jetting is often the difference between a clog that keeps returning every few months and one that actually stays clear, since it addresses the buildup and root growth along the whole line rather than just the blockage point.

How It Works.

What Hydro Jetting Actually Does

High-Pressure Water

A specialized nozzle sends water at high pressure through the line, scouring the full interior wall rather than punching a single hole through the clog.

Camera Verification

Before and after footage confirms the line is actually clear, not just flowing again temporarily.

Safe For Most Pipe Materials

Modern jetting equipment is calibrated for the pipe material found, whether PVC, cast iron, or older clay.

Root Cutting Attachments

Specialized nozzles can cut through root intrusion during the same pass that clears buildup.

Jetting Vs Snaking.

Which One Actually Fits Your Situation

Snaking is the right first step for most single, isolated clogs and it's faster and less expensive. Jetting is the better choice when the same clog keeps returning, when a kitchen line has years of grease buildup, or when a camera inspection shows root intrusion along the pipe wall rather than a single blockage point.

The Real Trade-Offs.

Snaking Vs Hydro Jetting

Cable Snaking

  • Faster and less expensive.
  • Clears the immediate blockage.
  • Best for a first-time, isolated clog.

Hydro Jetting

  • Clears the full interior pipe wall.
  • Removes grease buildup and root growth.
  • Better long-term fix for recurring clogs.
Pricing Factors.

What Affects Jetting Cost

Line LengthLonger runs take more time and water volume to fully clear.
Buildup SeverityYears of accumulated grease or root growth takes longer to clear than routine maintenance jetting.
Access PointAn existing cleanout speeds up the job compared to one that needs to be located first.
Camera InspectionOften paired with jetting to confirm the cause and verify the result.
Pipe Material And AgeOlder or more fragile pipe materials require calibrated pressure settings, which factors into scheduling and equipment choice.

Routine line maintenance, including jetting, is a homeowner cost, not an insurance matter. If buildup has already caused a backup that damaged the home's interior, that portion may be a separate, coverable claim.

After A Failed Snake.

What Jetting Adds When A Cable Has Already Fallen Short

Many people call us after a cable opened the drain for a few days and it clogged again. The cable pushed through the middle and left the wall coated.

Jetting uses pressurized water through a hose and a nozzle, which scours the whole inside of the pipe. We pair it with a camera so you can see what was in the line and how it looks afterward.

For a first-time clog or a single fixture, a cable is often enough. Our drain cleaning page covers that route.

Where It Fits Best.

Kitchen Lines, Branch Lines And Main Cleanouts

Jetting suits the pipes where buildup is the problem. That includes greasy kitchen lines, laundry branches and the main sewer line through a cleanout.

It can also clear a stubborn tub or toilet drain when a cable has not worked. If roots are involved, a cutting attachment may be used first. Once the line is open, a sewer repair discussion only comes up if the camera shows damage.

FAQs.

Common Questions

Is hydro jetting safe for older pipes?

Yes, when pressure is calibrated to the pipe material and condition. We assess this before jetting, often with a camera inspection first.

How often should a line be jetted?

It depends on use. A grease-heavy commercial kitchen line may benefit from routine scheduled jetting, while a typical residential line is jetted as needed rather than on a fixed schedule.

Will jetting damage tree roots I want to keep?

Jetting clears roots from inside the pipe only. It does not treat or kill the tree itself, so root intrusion can return if the tree's roots keep growing toward the same joint.

Do you verify the line is actually clear afterward?

Yes, with a camera inspection showing the line before and after.

Can hydro jetting clear a toilet or tub drain?

Yes, when a cable has failed or the clog keeps returning. We pick the method based on what the blockage is and what the pipe is made of.

Will jetting work from a main sewer cleanout?

Often yes. The cleanout gives direct access to the main line, and we confirm the result with a camera when possible.

How much does hydro jetting cost?

Pricing depends on pipe length, access and how heavy the buildup is. The dispatcher explains the structure of the quote on the phone, and jetting is typically priced differently from cable work.

Do you need to run a camera before jetting?

A camera before jetting shows whether the pipe can handle the pressure. It is not required for every job, but it is worth it on older lines or when something odd is suspected.
