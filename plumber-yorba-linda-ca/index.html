Local Plumbing Factors.

Why Yorba Linda Homes Call For A Different Approach

Homes From The 1970s Through The 1990s

Much of the housing was built in those decades, so original supply pipe, shutoff valves and water heaters are now well past their midlife. A pattern of repairs can point toward repiping.

Hillside Lots And Pressure

Houses lower on a slope see higher water pressure than those near the top. A regulator that has drifted can stress fixtures, so we read the pressure first.

Large Lots And Long Yard Lines

Bigger properties have longer supply and irrigation lines, and a leak in the yard can run for a while before it shows.

Pools And Irrigation

Many properties have pools and sprinkler systems, which brings backflow devices and equipment pad plumbing into the picture.

Emergency Prep.

If You Have A Burst Pipe Or Active Leak In Yorba Linda

Hillside plumbing can send water downhill before you notice it. Here is what to do first.

Shut Off The Main Valve

Find the main shutoff, often at the front of the house or near the garage, and turn it clockwise.

Cut Power To Wet Areas

If water reaches an outlet or panel, switch off that breaker and keep clear.

Check Downhill

Look below the leak on a slope or on a lower level where water may have collected.

Call Our 24/7 Line

Tell us whether the water comes from supply, drain or the water heater.
