Common Clogs.

Where Clogs Actually Happen

Kitchen Sink

Grease and food debris build up inside the trap and the line running to the main stack, especially in older homes with narrower pipe runs.

Shower Or Tub

Hair and soap scum combine into a mass that slows drainage gradually before stopping it completely.

Toilet

Most toilet clogs clear with a plunger or a closet auger. A toilet that clogs repeatedly often points to a partial blockage further down the line.

Main Sewer Line

Multiple fixtures backing up at once, especially the lowest drain in the house, usually means the clog is in the main line, not an individual fixture.

Why This Matters In Corona.

Older Sewer Lines And Root Intrusion

Corona's older neighborhoods still run original clay or cast iron sewer laterals in places. Mature landscaping near those lines means root intrusion is a real, recurring cause of a main line that clogs again a few weeks after snaking. A camera inspection settles the question quickly rather than repeating the same temporary fix on a schedule.

How We Clear It.

Snaking Vs Hydro Jetting

Cable Snaking

A drain snake breaks through and pulls back the immediate clog, the right first step for most single-fixture blockages.

Hydro Jetting

High-pressure water scours the full interior wall of the pipe, clearing grease buildup and root intrusion that a cable alone leaves behind.

Camera Inspection

For a clog that keeps coming back, a video camera shows exactly what is causing it, whether that is roots, a bellied pipe, or years of buildup.

When Jetting Is The Right Call

Recurring clogs, grease-heavy kitchen lines, and root intrusion in older clay or cast iron lines are the cases where jetting outperforms snaking alone.

Everything Backing Up At Once?

That usually means the main line, and it is treated as a priority emergency dispatch.

One Fixture Or The Whole House.

How To Tell What You're Dealing With

A single slow or clogged fixture is almost always local to that fixture's own line. When more than one fixture backs up at the same time, especially the lowest drain in the house, that points to the main line instead, and it changes both the urgency and the method we bring on the first visit.

If A Main Line Is Backing Up.

What To Do Before We Arrive

Stop Using Water In The House

Running more water down any drain while the main line is backed up pushes sewage further into the house instead of letting it settle.

Keep Contaminated Water Away From Kids And Pets

Sewage backup carries real bacterial risk. Treat any backed-up water as contaminated and keep people and animals away from it.

Skip Chemical Drain Cleaners On A Backup

Repeated chemical cleaner use on a main line clog does not clear a blockage that far down and can make the eventual repair more hazardous to work on.

Ventilate If There Is Sewage Odor

Open windows in the affected area if it is safe to do so. Persistent sewage odor indoors is a health concern worth treating seriously, not just an inconvenience.

FAQs.

Common Questions

Do you offer emergency same-day service?

Yes, our emergency line runs 24/7 for active backups, especially ones affecting multiple fixtures.

How do I know if I need hydro jetting instead of snaking?

If the clog returns within weeks of snaking, or if it is a main line with suspected grease or root buildup, jetting clears the full pipe wall instead of just punching through the immediate blockage.

Can you tell what's causing a recurring clog?

A video camera inspection shows the actual cause directly, roots, a bellied section, or buildup, rather than guessing.

Is a clogged toilet always a simple fix?

Usually, yes. A toilet that clogs repeatedly despite normal use is worth checking further down the line rather than just plunging it again.
