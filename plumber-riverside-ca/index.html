Emergency Prep.

If You Have A Burst Pipe Or Active Leak In Riverside

A burst pipe in an older Riverside home can run longer before anyone notices, especially on a large parcel. Here is what to do before the truck gets there.

Shut Off The Main Valve

Turn the main shutoff clockwise at the house. In older Arlington and La Sierra homes the valve can be stiff, so locate it now and do not force it with a tool that could snap it.

Cut Power To Wet Areas

If water is reaching an outlet, panel, or appliance, switch off that area at the breaker box before doing anything else nearby.

Clear The Immediate Area

Lift furniture and electronics out of the water while you wait, without delaying the first two steps.
