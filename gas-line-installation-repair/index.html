If You Smell Gas.

Take This Seriously, Every Time

Leave The Area Immediately

A gas odor is never something to investigate yourself. Get people and pets away from the area first.

Don't Use Switches Or Flames

Avoid light switches, appliances, phones, and any open flame near the suspected area.

Shut Off The Gas If You Can Safely Reach It

The main gas shutoff, if accessible without re-entering a risky area, stops the supply at the source.

Call From A Safe Distance

Contact us and, if there's any doubt about immediate danger, your gas utility's emergency line as well.

Who To Call First.

A Gas Smell Starts With Safety, Then Repair

If you smell gas, leave the house first. From outside, call your gas utility's emergency line or 911. Do not hunt for the source indoors.

We handle the plumbing side after the area is safe. That means locating the leak, repairing or replacing the damaged section and testing the line before it goes back into service.

Warning signs include a rotten-egg smell, a hissing sound near a line, dead patches of grass over a buried line and appliances that act oddly.

Why This Matters In Corona.

Older Gas Lines And Growing Outdoor Living

Corona's older homes sometimes run original gas piping that predates current sizing and material standards, which is worth checking before adding a new appliance to an existing line. At the same time, the popularity of outdoor kitchens and fire features in Corona's climate means new gas line installation for outdoor use is a genuinely common request here, not just an occasional one. All gas work follows standard UPC and California gas code requirements for sizing, venting, and shutoff placement.

New Installation Vs Repair.

Two Different Conversations

Adding a line for a new appliance is a planned project we can schedule and quote in advance. A suspected leak is treated as an urgent safety matter regardless of when you call. Both are licensed gas work, but they move through our scheduling very differently.

What Not To Do.

Before Help Arrives

Know Your Gas Meter Shutoff

The main gas shutoff at the meter, typically turned a quarter turn with a wrench, stops gas to the entire property in an emergency.

Never Use A Flame To Check For A Leak

Soapy water applied to a suspected joint will bubble if gas is escaping. An open flame is never a safe way to check.

Watch For Physical Warning Signs

A hissing sound near a gas line, dead vegetation in an otherwise healthy area over a buried line, or a persistent sulfur-like smell all warrant a call.

Schedule Periodic Checks On Older Systems

A home with original gas piping benefits from an occasional inspection even without an active complaint, especially before adding new appliances.

Repair Vs New InstallLeak repair scope depends on the leak's location and accessibility.

New gas line installation is a homeowner improvement cost, not an insurance matter. A gas leak that caused damage or required emergency response may have a separate insurance conversation worth having with your provider.

New Hookups.

Stoves, Fireplaces, Fire Pits And Outdoor Kitchens

Many gas jobs are planned upgrades. A kitchen remodel may need a new stove line. A patio project may need a fire pit or grill connection.

Gas fireplace and insert lines follow the same logic. We also convert appliances between natural gas and propane. The conversion kit and the appliance model decide the scope, and the technician confirms both before starting.

Installed lines are pressure tested before use. Related appliance work is on our water heater installation page and tankless service page.

FAQs.

Common Questions

Is gas line work permitted work?

Yes, in most cases, and we handle the permit and inspection process as part of the project.

How quickly can you respond to a suspected leak?

This is treated as a priority safety call, not standard scheduling.

Can you add a line for an outdoor fire pit or kitchen?

Yes, this is a common request, properly sized and installed to code for outdoor use.

Do you test the line after installation?

Yes, every new or repaired line is pressure-tested before being put into service.

Do you inspect existing gas lines during other plumbing work?

We can, on request, especially in older homes where the gas piping predates current sizing standards.

Can you convert a natural gas line to propane, or vice versa?

This typically requires appliance-specific conversion kits alongside any line work, and we can discuss what your specific appliances need.

Should I call the gas company or a plumber first?

Call your gas utility's emergency line or 911 first, from outside the house. We repair the line after the area has been made safe.

Can you hook up a gas stove or a dryer?

Yes. A new appliance hookup is a standard job, and the line is tested before it is used.

Do you run gas lines for fireplaces and fire pits?

Yes. Gas fireplaces, inserts and outdoor fire features are common requests. The dispatcher confirms the details of your setup before scheduling.

Will my gas stay off during a repair?

The affected line is shut off while it is repaired. Other appliances may stay on or off depending on how the system is laid out, and the technician tells you before starting.
