Plumbing In Chino

Chino sits in the Chino Valley, about 15 road miles northwest of Corona. We reach it from Corona on SR-71, and our 24/7 line covers the whole city, including ZIPs 91708 and 91710.

Local Plumbing Factors.

Why Chino Homes Call For A Different Approach

Older Downtown Housing

Homes near downtown Chino include many built decades ago. Original supply pipe, drain lines and shutoff valves in those houses are the first places we look when a leak pattern shows up.

The Preserve And Newer Tracts

Newer master-planned neighborhoods run on modern piping and larger water heaters. Problems there tend to be fixtures, valves and water heater settings rather than failing pipe.

Former Dairy And Rural Edges

Some properties near former dairy land are larger and may run septic systems or long yard lines. We cover septic pumping and buried line work for those lots.

Hard Water Across The Valley

Water in this part of the Inland Empire tends toward the hard side. Scale shortens water heater life and clogs fixtures, so descaling and softening come up often.

Emergency Prep.

If You Have A Burst Pipe Or Active Leak In Chino

A large lot or an older slab home can hide water for a while before it shows. Here is what to do first.

Shut Off The Main Valve

Find the main shutoff near the front of the house or the garage and turn it clockwise.

Cut Power To Wet Areas

If water is near an outlet or panel, switch off that breaker first and stay clear.

Check Low Spots

Water runs to the lowest point. Look along baseboards and under cabinets for pooling.
