Common Reasons Households Ask.

What Filtration Actually Addresses

Taste Or Odor Concerns

Chlorine taste or smell from municipal treatment is one of the most common reasons households look into filtration.

Wanting To Reduce Reliance On Bottled Water

A whole-home or dedicated drinking water system can replace the ongoing cost and waste of bottled water.

Specific Contaminant Concerns

Households with a specific concern, from sediment to particular compounds, can be matched to a filtration type that actually addresses it.

Sensitive Skin Or Household Members

Some households pursue filtration for skin sensitivity reasons distinct from hardness, which a softener alone doesn't address.

Filtration Vs Softening, Not The Same Thing.

Why This Is A Separate Service From Water Softening

Corona's hard water is a mineral hardness issue, addressed by a water softener. Filtration is a different, complementary concern, taste, odor, sediment, and specific water quality factors that a softener alone doesn't touch. Many Corona households benefit from both, a softener to protect appliances and fixtures, and filtration for the water they actually drink and cook with.

System Types.

Matching The System To The Actual Concern

Whole-Home Filtration

Treats water at the point it enters the house, addressing chlorine taste, sediment, and general water quality throughout every fixture.

Under-Sink Drinking Water Systems

A more targeted, often more thorough filtration specifically for drinking and cooking water at one location.

Reverse Osmosis

The most thorough filtration option for a dedicated drinking water tap, removing a broad range of dissolved substances.

Sediment Pre-Filters

Often paired with other filtration types to protect downstream equipment, including a water softener, from particulate buildup.

Filtration Vs Softening.

How To Tell Which One You Actually Need

If your concern is spotting, scale, or appliance wear, that's a hardness issue and a softener is the right answer. If your concern is taste, odor, or specific water quality, that's filtration. Many households want both, and they're not competing systems, they solve different problems working together.

A Note On Water Quality Concerns.

When To Look Beyond Standard Filtration

Municipal Water Reports Are Public

Corona's annual water quality report is publicly available and worth reviewing if you have a specific contaminant concern before choosing a system.

Well Water Is A Different Conversation

Properties on well water rather than municipal supply need water testing first, since the concerns and appropriate systems differ meaningfully.

Some Concerns Need Specialized Systems

A specific documented contaminant concern, rather than general taste or odor, may call for a system matched specifically to that substance rather than general-purpose filtration.

Filters Need Maintenance To Keep Working

An expired filter cartridge can stop protecting effectively well before it visibly clogs, which is why a replacement schedule matters as much as the system itself.

Pricing Factors.

What Affects Cost

System TypeWhole-home systems generally cost more upfront than a single under-sink unit.
Filtration LevelReverse osmosis systems cost more than basic carbon filtration due to added components.
Household Water UsageHigher usage may call for a higher-capacity system to avoid frequent filter changes.
Installation ComplexityWhole-home systems require main line installation; under-sink systems are generally simpler.
Ongoing Filter CostsDifferent system types have different filter replacement schedules and costs worth factoring into the decision.

Water filtration installation is a homeowner improvement cost, not an insurance matter.

FAQs.

Common Questions

Do I need both a softener and a filtration system?

Not necessarily, but many households benefit from both since they address different concerns, hardness versus taste and quality.

How often do filters need to be replaced?

It varies by system type and household usage, typically ranging from a few months to about a year for most residential systems.

Is reverse osmosis worth it for a whole house?

Usually not cost-effective at whole-house scale. It's most commonly installed as a dedicated drinking water tap instead.

Will filtration remove the chlorine taste from my water?

Yes, that's one of the most straightforward things basic carbon filtration addresses well.

How is filtered water different from bottled water?

A properly maintained filtration system can match or exceed bottled water quality at a fraction of the ongoing cost and without the packaging waste.
