Ceilings And Walls.

Stains On The Ceiling Or A Damp Wall

A brown stain on a ceiling is not always a plumbing leak. A roof leak can look the same. A stain below a bathroom, near a pipe run or under an upstairs sink points to plumbing.

Dampness on a wall can come from a supply line, a drain line or a fitting inside the cavity. A fridge water line that splits behind the appliance can also flood a kitchen floor. We trace the water back to the source before anyone cuts into drywall.

Crawlspaces.

Leaks Under The House

Homes on raised foundations have a crawlspace where pipes run in the open. A leak down there can go unseen for months. Warning signs include musty smells, a sagging floor or a water bill that creeps up.

Technicians check access first. We inspect supply lines, drain connections and the underside of fixtures. For leaks that are hard to locate, our leak detection page explains the tools. Slab homes follow a different path, covered on the slab leak page.

Pressure.

Low Water Pressure Or No Water At All

Weak pressure at every tap suggests a problem at the main valve, the pressure regulator or the supply line. Weak pressure at one fixture is usually a clogged aerator or a partly closed valve. Our low water pressure page covers gauge testing and repairs in detail.

No water at all means checking the main valve, then looking for a break. Old galvanized pipe also loses flow from the inside, which is covered on our repiping page.

FAQs.

Common Questions

What if I don't know what's actually wrong?

That's fine. Describe what you're noticing, sounds, smells, symptoms, and we'll take it from there.

Can you address multiple issues in one visit?

Yes, that's exactly what this service is for, and it's usually more efficient than separate calls.

What if my issue turns out to be bigger than expected?

We'll tell you honestly and quote the actual scope before doing any additional work beyond what we discussed.

Do you offer free estimates for this kind of call?

Yes, for non-emergency general repair visits.

Do you work with home warranty companies?

We can discuss this on a case-by-case basis when you call, since coverage and process vary by provider.

Is a ceiling stain a plumbing leak or a roof leak?

Either is possible. A stain that grows during rain suggests the roof. A stain that appears below a bathroom or after fixture use suggests plumbing.

Can you check under my house for a leak?

Yes. We inspect the crawlspace for supply and drain leaks. Tell the dispatcher where access is located.

Why did my water pressure suddenly drop?

A partly closed main valve, a failing pressure regulator or a leak can all cause it. A quick check at several fixtures narrows it down.

My refrigerator water line leaked onto the floor. Can you fix it?

Yes. We replace the line and the valve behind the fridge, then check for any hidden moisture.

Can you add a new line or a fixture?

Yes. New fixtures, hookups and small additions are standard work. The dispatcher explains what the job involves.
