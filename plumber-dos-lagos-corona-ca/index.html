Verified Facts.

What We Can Confirm About Dos Lagos

  • Dos Lagos is a specific plan area annexed into the City of Corona as Annexation No. 94.
  • An engineering firm that worked on the plan describes it as 458 acres.
  • The plan includes residential areas, condominiums and a golf resort.
  • Temescal Canyon Road runs through the area, and the city has a road widening project for the Dos Lagos segment.
  • Listing sites file most Dos Lagos homes under ZIP 92883.

These facts come from planning records and listing sites. Lot-level boundaries are not something we have verified.

General Plumbing Information.

Is Your Address In Corona Or Temescal Valley?

Dos Lagos sits close to the unincorporated community of Temescal Valley, and mailing names can blur the line. For a plumbing call the boundary matters less than the street address, because that is what we use to plan the trip and to find the water meter.

Condominiums and attached homes bring a shared-pipe question. A leak in a ceiling below another unit, or a clog that backs up several homes, is usually a shared-line problem. A sewer camera inspection shows where the blockage or break sits.

For detached homes, a slow drain in one fixture is a branch problem, while slow drains in several fixtures point to the main line. That simple pattern saves time when you describe the issue on the phone.
