Signs You Need One.

How Hard Water Shows Up At Home

Spots On Dishes And Glassware

White mineral spots that won't rinse away, even right out of the dishwasher, are a classic hard water sign.

Soap That Won't Lather Well

Hard water reacts with soap to form a film rather than a full lather, leaving skin and hair feeling different after washing.

Scale Buildup On Fixtures

Chalky white buildup around faucets and showerheads is mineral scale accumulating from the water itself.

Appliances Wearing Out Faster

Water heaters, dishwashers, and washing machines all see shortened lifespans from ongoing mineral buildup inside their components.

Why This Matters In Corona.

Corona's Water Runs Moderately Hard

Corona's municipal water supply runs moderately hard, which is the direct cause of the scale buildup, spotting, and shortened appliance life described above. A properly sized softener addresses this at the source, extending the working life of everything downstream of it, including the water heater, which is one of the more expensive appliances hard water tends to wear out early. Installation follows standard UPC plumbing code requirements for backflow prevention at the softener's connection point.

Repair Or Replace.

When Each Makes Sense

Common Repairable Issues

A worn resin bed, a stuck bypass valve, or a control head malfunction are all repairable on most existing systems.

When Replacement Makes More Sense

A system past 10-15 years old, or one that's been through several repairs already, is often better value replaced than kept limping along.

Sizing A New System

Household size and water usage set the right capacity. An undersized system runs constantly and wears out faster.

Salt-Based Vs Salt-Free

Traditional salt-based systems remove hardness minerals directly, while salt-free conditioners alter mineral behavior without removing them, each with real trade-offs worth discussing for your household. Salt-based systems generally perform more thoroughly on genuinely hard water, while salt-free options appeal to households wanting to avoid sodium addition or ongoing salt purchases.

Is It Worth It.

What A Softener Actually Saves You

Beyond spotting and soap film, a softener meaningfully extends the working life of a water heater, dishwasher, and washing machine, all of which accumulate scale internally without one. For most Corona households, the appliance and plumbing longevity alone offsets a meaningful share of the system's cost over its lifetime.

Not Softening.

Softener Running But The Water Is Still Hard

A softener that runs but does not soften has a few usual causes. The salt may have bridged, the resin may be worn, the control valve may be failing or the unit may be set wrong.

Check that salt is present and loose. If the problem remains, a valve replacement or a resin check may be needed. Softeners and filters do different jobs, so see our filtration page if taste or odor is the issue.

FAQs.

Common Questions

How do I know if my water is actually hard?

We can test it directly, though visible scale, spotting, and soap film are strong practical indicators on their own.

Is salt-free as effective as salt-based?

They work differently. Salt-based systems remove hardness minerals outright, while salt-free systems condition them so they don't stick to surfaces as readily. We can walk through which fits your household's priorities.

How often does a softener need maintenance?

Salt-based systems need periodic salt refills and occasional resin bed service. We can set expectations for your specific system during installation.

Can a softener be added to an existing home without major work?

In most cases, yes. It typically installs at the main water line entry point without extensive re-plumbing.

Will a softener affect my water pressure?

A properly sized system has minimal impact on pressure. An undersized system is the more common cause of pressure complaints, which is why sizing matters more than picking the cheapest available unit.

My softener runs but the water is still hard. Why?

Salt bridging, worn resin or a failing control valve are the common causes. A short visit finds which one.

Can you replace just the control valve?

In many cases yes, when the tank itself is sound.

Do you work on the softener I already own?

Often yes. Tell the dispatcher the make and model so they can confirm.

Local Neighborhoods.

Softener Placement By Neighborhood

Corona's municipal water is moderately hard everywhere, so the real question is which system fits the home. Large homes in Eagle Glen and Sycamore Creek often have garage space for a full-size softener. Older homes in Home Gardens or near downtown may need a different layout, and some associations have rules about visible equipment, so we check before we plan the install.
