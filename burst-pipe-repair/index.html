What To Do In The First Few Minutes

Shut Off The Main Water Valve

This is the single most important step. It stops the flooding immediately while help is on the way.

Cut Power To Affected Areas

If water is near an outlet, panel, or appliance, shut off power to that circuit at the breaker box before touching anything.

Move Valuables Away From Water

Furniture, electronics, and belongings moved off a wet floor are far easier to save the sooner they are out of standing water.

Call Immediately, Don't Wait

The longer a burst pipe runs, even after the main is off, the more water has already spread into flooring and walls.

Why This Happens In Corona.

Aging Copper Under Real Pressure

A meaningful share of Corona's housing stock runs 50-plus-year-old copper supply lines. A sudden burst is often the end result of years of slow corrosion finally giving way, sometimes triggered by a pressure spike or ground shift rather than any single obvious cause. Homes with a whole-house pressure regulator already installed see fewer sudden bursts than ones running at full municipal pressure continuously.

Our Response.

What Happens When We Arrive

Confirm The Main Is Off

First priority is making sure water flow has actually stopped before assessing the break itself.

Locate And Assess The Break

We identify exactly where the pipe failed and whether it is an isolated break or a sign of broader line condition issues.

Repair Or Replace The Section

Most breaks are repaired by replacing the damaged section rather than the entire line, unless the pipe's overall condition warrants more.

Restore Water Service

Once repaired, the system is pressure-tested before water service is fully restored to confirm the fix holds.

Water Actively Spraying Or Flooding?

Repair Vs Restoration.

Two Different Jobs After A Burst Pipe

Fixing the pipe itself is what this page covers. If the burst has already soaked flooring, drywall, or insulation, that cleanup and drying is a separate job, water damage restoration, which we can coordinate at the same time rather than making you find a second company.

Pricing Factors.

What Affects Burst Pipe Repair Cost

Access To The BreakA pipe behind an accessible wall or under a sink costs less to reach than one under a slab or in a tight crawlspace.
Pipe MaterialCopper, PEX, and galvanized repairs use different fittings and techniques.
Extent Of The DamageA single clean break costs less than a section showing corrosion along its length.
After-Hours ResponseEmergency dispatch outside standard hours is priced differently from a scheduled daytime repair.
Water Damage ScopeIf restoration is also needed, that is quoted separately from the plumbing repair itself.

A sudden burst pipe is commonly a covered peril under homeowner insurance, unlike a slow, long-term leak. We document the break and repair for your claim either way.

Cracked Or Burst.

A Hairline Crack Can Be As Urgent As A Full Burst

Not every emergency looks like a geyser. A cracked elbow, a split coupling or a pinhole that sprays a fine mist can move a lot of water in an hour.

Calls we hear often involve a cracked water line fitting, a failed pump connection or a broken supply valve. The fix is usually a section replacement, not a patch. We confirm the pipe on both sides of the break is sound before closing it up, because a weak neighbor is the next leak.

Ceilings And Attics.

Water Coming Through A Ceiling Or Dripping From The Attic

Stains spreading across a ceiling mean water is traveling along framing before it shows. The visible spot is rarely the source.

Close The Main First

Stopping the supply is the fastest way to slow the stain.

Keep Clear Of Sagging Drywall

A bulging ceiling can drop without warning, so stay out from under it.

Switch Off Nearby Power

If water is near light fixtures, turn off that circuit at the panel.

Take Photos

Wide and close shots help the technician trace the path back to the break.

Stuck Valves.

When The Main Shutoff Will Not Close

A main valve that will not turn, or keeps dripping when closed, is a problem in itself. Old gate valves seize with age.

A utility-side shutoff at the meter box can sometimes stop the flow, but that equipment belongs to the utility. Ask Corona Department of Water & Power before using it. Once the leak is stopped, we can replace the seized valve so the next emergency has a working shutoff. Fixture-level valves are covered on our faucet and sink repair page. If bursts keep returning, see repiping.

FAQs.

Common Questions

How fast can you get here?

This is treated as a priority emergency dispatch, 24/7, including nights, weekends, and holidays.

Do I need to find the main shutoff myself?

If you can, yes, it meaningfully limits damage before we arrive. If you can't locate it, tell us on the phone and we'll walk you through it.

Will you also handle the water damage?

We handle the plumbing repair directly. Water damage cleanup is coordinated through our restoration line so both get addressed without you managing two separate companies.

Is a burst pipe always a sign of a bigger problem?

Not always, but it's worth having the surrounding line's condition checked, especially in an older home, since one failure can be an early sign of others.

What if my main shutoff valve is stuck or leaking?

Tell the dispatcher when you call. A seized valve can often be worked around, and we can replace it once the leak is stopped so the home has a shutoff that closes.

Is a cracked pipe fitting treated as an emergency?

If it is spraying or pooling water, yes. Even a slow leak at a fitting can saturate walls, so we treat active water as urgent until it is stopped.

Can a leak in the attic be fixed in one visit?

Often the repair itself is quick once the break is found. Tracing the source through framing takes longer, and the dispatcher tells you what to expect when you call.

Should I repair one burst or replace the whole line?

A single break in sound pipe is usually a section repair. Several breaks in a short time suggest the pipe is failing, and that is when a repipe is worth pricing out.

Local Neighborhoods.

Burst Pipes Across Corona's Terrain

A burst line in an older central Corona or Home Gardens home usually traces to aging supply pipe. Hillside homes in Sierra del Oro and Eagle Glen can also see pressure swings from elevation that strain fixtures and valves over time. After any burst we check static pressure, because a failing regulator can set up the next break even after the first one is fixed.
