Home Zone.

Corona And The Communities Beside It

The closest communities share our daily routes with Corona itself, and none is more than about five road miles from our base. Home Gardens sits just east of the city along SR-91, El Cerrito lies to the southeast, and Norco, the Horsetown USA city, is just to the north.

Home Gardens

An unincorporated community of about 11,200 residents inside Corona's sphere of influence, roughly 3 miles east of downtown. Mid-century streets sit beside later infill, so home age varies block to block.

El Cerrito

A hillside community of about 5,000 residents southeast of Corona, with large lots, equestrian zoning, and a mix of septic and city sewer.

Norco

Horsetown USA, a city of roughly 27,000 about 5 miles north, with big lots, barns, and no sidewalks by design.

North And Northeast.

Eastvale And Jurupa Valley

Up the I-15 corridor, Eastvale and Jurupa Valley are two of the largest markets we serve. They sit between Corona and the San Bernardino County line, and their housing could hardly be more different.

Eastvale

A city of about 69,800 residents roughly 8 miles north, incorporated in 2010 and built mostly since the late 1990s. ZIPs 92880 and 91752.

View Details →

Jurupa Valley

A city of more than 100,000 formed in 2011 from nine communities. Mira Loma is about 10 road miles out, and Glen Avon and Rubidoux about 14. ZIPs 91752 and 92509.

Inside Corona.

Corona's Own Neighborhoods Need No Separate Pages

A neighborhood inside Corona is a different thing from a separate community outside it. Sierra del Oro, Corona Hills, Corona Ranch, Eagle Glen, Dos Lagos, Horsethief Canyon Ranch, Sycamore Creek, Brentridge, Green River, and the downtown Circle are all part of our home city, so every service page on this site already covers them.

Smaller Communities.

Covered On The Same Routes

Coronita, with about 2,600 residents, El Sobrante, with about 14,000, and Lake Mathews, with about 6,000, are unincorporated communities that border Corona or sit between Corona and Riverside. Woodcrest lies just south of Riverside. They share plumbing conditions with their neighbors, so we cover them on the same routes instead of giving each its own page.

Places just beyond these areas, such as Lake Elsinore and Ontario, are handled case by case. Call and we will tell you plainly whether we can reach you.

Response Times.

Honest About Distance

Our 24/7 emergency line covers every area on this page. Communities in the home zone are served on the same daily routes as central Corona. For Jurupa Valley, Riverside, Chino Hills, and Temescal Valley, arrival depends on distance and freeway traffic, so you get an honest estimate instead of a flat promise.

Housing Age By Area.

A Quick Reference

Older Housing Stock

Central Corona and Home Gardens carry most of the older construction, with the aging copper and galvanized supply lines that come with it.

The 1980s And 1990s

Sierra del Oro and Chino Hills were built up mostly in the late 1980s and early 1990s, so original plumbing is now 30 to 40 years old.

Newer Construction

South Corona, Eastvale, Temescal Valley, and newer Mira Loma tracts have been built out mostly since the late 1990s, meaning newer copper or PEX plumbing and different common issues than older neighborhoods.

Large-Lot And Equestrian Properties

El Cerrito and Norco both feature large lots built for horses, with longer line runs and barn plumbing considerations. La Sierra Acres in Riverside has similarly large parcels.

A Mix Of Eras

Jurupa Valley merged nine communities, and west Riverside runs from Arlington's 1909 library to Orangecrest, annexed in 1986, so we ask about the home before guessing.

Cities And Communities.

City Lines Don't Change Our Standard

Home Gardens, El Cerrito, and Temescal Valley are unincorporated Riverside County communities. Norco, Eastvale, Jurupa Valley, and Riverside are Riverside County cities, and Chino Hills is a city in San Bernardino County. Either way, the same technicians, upfront pricing, and 24/7 emergency line apply. Our full services page lists every repair, installation, and diagnostic service we perform.
